Frequently Asked Questions

  • Where does job data come from? — All listings are fetched directly from Arbetsförmedlingen (the Swedish Public Employment Service) via the JobTech API.
  • How do I save a job? — Tap the bookmark icon on any job listing to save it. Saved jobs are stored locally on your device.
  • How do I remove ads? — Go to Settings and tap "Remove Ads" to make a one-time in-app purchase.
  • How do I change the language? — Go to Settings and select your preferred language.
  • My saved jobs disappeared. — Saved jobs are stored locally. Reinstalling the app or clearing app data will remove them.
